1. Which robot should I use?

Every formation carries one robot. Choose by battle mode and the robot's War Skill.

Shooter · Cage & rallies

Phantom Cat

Best for: Shooter-heavy rallies and Trial Cage.

Max War Skill: +37.5% Lethality for every troop. Every 4 Shooter attacks trigger an extra +80% Tactical DMG.

Why: Its Shooter ATK/DEF increases and Tactical hits do much of the work in Shooter-heavy fights.

Max pilot skill: +10% HP for expedition troops, 2 hours.

Shooter · Solo attacks

Musashimaru

Best for: Solo attacks with Shooter-heavy marches.

Max War Skill: Shieldbearers and Shooters gain +30% Lethality and take 45% less damage.

Why: Solo armies run 0–5% Bombers, so the skill covers nearly the entire army and its damage reduction stays active.

Max pilot skill: Enemy DEF −10%, 2 hours.

Bomber · Defense

Bastion

Best for: Defensive lineups, especially Bomber-oriented defenses.

Max War Skill: Every troop gains +40% DEF, +35% Lethality and +50% Tactical DMG, plus +230% more (280% total) on rounds not divisible by 3.

Why: The Tactical DMG and Bomber-wall defenses pair well with a Bomber ATK/DEF increase on the garrison.

Max pilot skill: Enemy ATK −10%, 2 hours.
